Quick Start Guide

Welcome to EZRAlms. Here is how to get started with your daily tasks.

1

Catalog Books

Go to 'Books' and add new titles with ISBN and available copies.

2

Register Members

Add students or staff in the 'Members' section to start lending.

3

Issue Loans

Use 'New Loan' to link a book to a member with a due date.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I return a book?
Navigate to the 'Library Loan' page. In the 'Active' or 'Overdue' tabs, click the 'Return' button next to the relevant loan record.
What if a member loses their ID?
You can search for members by their full name or email address in the 'Members' section to find their internal ID and loan history.
Can I export the catalog?
Currently, data is managed within the dashboard. Direct CSV export features are planned for future updates.
How are overdue loans calculated?
The system automatically flags any active loan whose 'Due Date' is in the past as 'Overdue'. These appear highlighted in red on the Dashboard.
